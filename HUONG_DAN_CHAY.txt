================================================================================

1. CAI DAT MOI TRUONG & THU VIEN:
   Mo Terminal (Command Prompt / PowerShell) tai thu muc nay va chay lenh:
   
   pip install -r requirements.txt

2. KHOI CHAY UNG DUNG:
   - Cach 1 (Nhanh nhat tren Windows): 
     Nhap dup chuot vao file: run_gui.bat
     
   - Cach 2 (Bang dong lenh):
     python asl-sign-language/gui_app.py

3. CHAY KIEM THU TU DONG (INTEGRATION TEST):
   python run_full_system_test.py
